# Page pictures for the Spanish trainer

Save each finished picture in this folder (`assets/pages/`) under the file name given below. It then appears automatically in that page's banner. Until a picture exists, the page shows the shared workshop picture.

**Format for every picture**
- Wide landscape, 2:1 (for example 1774 × 887).
- The blue monster and the main objects sit in the **left half**.
- The **right 45 %** is quiet and dark (deep midnight blue or teal shadow), because the page title is written there.

**Shared style (repeat it in every prompt)**

> Rich intricate storybook fantasy painting, the same magical illustrated world as the reference images. One friendly fluffy electric-blue fantasy MONSTER, not a cat: round furry body, very large warm amber eyes, small peach nose, pointed ears with orange inner ears, long soft blue fur, small clawed paws. Tactile fur, wood, paper, leather and moss. Saturated jewel colours: midnight blue, deep teal, oxblood red, warm amber lantern light, restrained gold. Little red-and-white toadstools and red flowers at the edges, as in the mushroom-forest references. Friendly, curious expression. No writing, lettering, numbers, labels or logos anywhere. No neon, no interface mockup, no black cut-out background. Finished production illustration.

## practice.png · Verbos y tiempos
> [Shared style] Scene: the blue monster sits at a carved wooden desk in an enchanted library at night, sorting glowing puzzle pieces into three small wooden trays. Beside it stand a large brass hourglass with amber sand, a small antique clock and a stack of red and green leather books. The pieces drift gently between yesterday (an autumn leaf), today (a cup of steaming coffee) and tomorrow (a small sprouting seed). Toadstools grow from the bookshelf. Monster and objects on the left; the right side is dark shelves fading into shadow.

## say.png · ¿Cómo se dice…?
> [Shared style] Scene: a Spanish café terrace at dusk in an old town square, with warm lanterns, a wrought-iron balcony and terracotta pots of red geraniums. The blue monster sits at a small round marble table with a café con leche and a plate of churros, and lifts one paw as if starting to say something to a friendly waiter. Small blank cream speech cards float in the air like butterflies, with no text on them. Toadstools at the foot of the table. Monster on the left; the right side is the dark blue evening sky over rooftops.

## mean.png · ¿Qué significa?
> [Shared style] Scene: the blue monster at a busy evening Spanish market stall, holding a large brass magnifying glass up to its eye and listening carefully, ears tilted. Around it: baskets of oranges, strings of garlic, hand-painted blue-and-white azulejo tiles without letters, and a vintage cream telephone on the counter. A small toadstool grows between the crates. Monster on the left; the right side is the darkening market hall with soft lantern glow.

## rules.png · Entender la diferencia
> [Shared style] Scene: in a moonlit mushroom forest, a mossy path splits into two. The blue monster stands at the fork holding an old brass balance scale; on one pan glows a small blue lantern, on the other a small amber lantern, perfectly balanced. Two carved wooden signposts with blank boards. Giant red toadstools with white spots frame the left edge. Monster on the left; the right side is misty teal forest fading into darkness.

## reading.png · Lectura e ideas
> [Shared style] Scene: a cosy reading nook in an Andalusian courtyard at night. The blue monster is curled up on a cushion under an orange tree, reading a large open storybook whose illustrated pages glow softly with tiny scenes rising from them: a little ship, a mountain village and a train. Next to it are a tiled fountain, a brass lantern and a pile of books. Small toadstools in the flower pots. Monster on the left; the right side is a dark blue night sky with a few stars.

## family.png · Conversaciones en familia
> [Shared style] Scene: a warm Spanish family dinner, the sobremesa after the meal, in a rustic dining room with a hanging lamp. The blue monster sits at a long wooden table with a small family of blue monsters of different sizes: an older grandmother monster with tiny round glasses and a little monster child. They chat happily around a big paella pan, bread, oranges and a jug of water. Red geraniums in the window and a few toadstools in a clay pot. Group on the left; the right side is the dim room fading into warm shadow.
